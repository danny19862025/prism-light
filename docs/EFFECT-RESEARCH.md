# Expanded effect research — 13 November 2026

The public SignalRGB Marketplace was reviewed through its visible Home and RGB Effects pages. Visible catalog examples included Waterpaint, Cyberspace, Fireflies, Aurora, Arctic, Azure, Voronoi, Watercolor, and audio visualizers. SignalRGB's official Discover documentation also illustrates Matrix, and its customization guide illustrates Rain and adjustable color, speed, scale and direction.

Sources:

- [Public Marketplace](https://marketplace.signalrgb.com/)
- [Discover documentation](https://docs.signalrgb.com/quick-start/discover/)
- [Customization documentation](https://docs.signalrgb.com/guides/effects-customization/how-to-customize-effects/)
- [Lightscripts canvas model](https://docs.signalrgb.com/lightscripts)

The marketplace was used as visual-category research. Prism Light's twenty additional effects are independently written mathematical RGB renderers with locally generated thumbnails. They are not imports of SignalRGB effect code and do not reproduce its account, marketplace, paid-content or game-integration functionality. No account sign-in, local-network permission grant, effect purchase or storefront download was performed.
